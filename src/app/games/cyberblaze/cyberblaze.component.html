Cyberblaze

Defend the data stream!

Cyberblaze is a wave defense shooter in which you are left to fend for yourself, fighting off swarms off enemies with a varied arsenal. You must use everything at your disposal to defend the data stream, and can choose from a wide variety of munitions to fend off the growing horde.

Featuring a varied roster of enemies, weaponry, a simplistic, blocky art style and a progression system that rewards you with XP and levelling to further bolster your power.
