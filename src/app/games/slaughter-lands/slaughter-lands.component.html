Slaughter Lands

How long can you survive?

Slaughter Lands is a Call Of Duty Zombies inspired game, in which the player must survive against hordes of the undead.

This game holds a special (if janky) place in my heart, as it is the first Unity game I ever made. It was in development since 2015, when I was 13. And has gone over many, MANY iterations.

The game is no longer in development due to a variety of factors; primarily due to a desire to move onto new projects, on top of upgrading Unity, which broke the entire game's codebase as it was previously written in JavaScript.
