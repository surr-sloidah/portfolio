The Fumbling Wizard

Delay the inevitable!

The Fumbling Wizard is a short game developed for Ludum Dare 50!
You must protect the world from the impending meteor for as long as possible. You must cast a spell to keep it at bay......but someone mixed up the words in your spellbook.
You must quickly flick between the pages to find the correct word and give us all just a little bit more time, uttering the wrong word will however bring us closer to our doom.
And that's not even all! The spell requires potions, and their effects may not be the healthiest.
